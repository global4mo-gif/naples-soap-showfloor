Fair questions

Three questions you may be asking

Will this take sales from our stores and wholesale?

The buyer here is someone in another state who has never heard of you. It is a new audience — and the one growing fastest.

Can our margin carry creator commission?

We launch only products whose margin survives fees and shipping, and lead with bundles like the $28 hair bar set. The rest of the catalogue stays where it is.

We already run ads. Why add this?

Ads charge for attention. Affiliates charge only for sales — and hand you the creator content your ads are missing.
